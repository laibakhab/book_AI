# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics — Essentials

Welcome to your comprehensive textbook on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book provides a complete introduction to the cutting-edge technologies driving the next generation of intelligent robotics.

## About This Book

This textbook is organized into six comprehensive chapters:

1. **Physical AI** - Understanding the fundamentals of embodied artificial intelligence
2. **Humanoid Robotics** - Exploring the design and mechanics of human-like robots
3. **ROS 2** - Mastering the Robot Operating System for robotics development
4. **Simulation** - Building and testing robotic systems in virtual environments
5. **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Integrating perception, cognition, and action
6. **Capstone Project** - Applying your knowledge to a complete robotics challenge

Each chapter contains theoretical foundations, practical examples, and hands-on exercises to reinforce your learning. Start with Chapter 1 and progress sequentially for the best learning experience.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this book, you will:
- Understand the core concepts of Physical AI
- Be familiar with humanoid robot design principles
- Have practical experience with ROS 2 development
- Know how to develop and test robots in simulation
- Understand Vision-Language-Action systems
- Complete a capstone project integrating all concepts
